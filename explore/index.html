Walk from Two Seas

Government Botanical Garden

About 700 m · 10-min walk

Laid out in 1847 and maintained by the Tamil Nadu Horticulture Department. Terraced lawns, a fossil tree trunk and an Italian garden. Go early morning for the quietest visit.

St. Stephen's Church

About 1.5 km

One of the oldest churches in the Nilgiris (1829), with timber said to come from Tipu Sultan's palace in Srirangapatna.

Open in Google Maps →

Government Rose Garden

About 1.2 km · short drive

Thousands of rose varieties on terraced slopes, best from April to June and again after the monsoon.

Half-Day & Day Trips

Emerald Lake

About 18–20 km · 45–55 min

A calm, blue lake in the Silent Valley area, ringed by tea gardens and forest slopes. Lovely for photos, bird watching and quiet walks. There are no eateries nearby, so carry water and snacks. Pair it with Avalanche Lake, about 5 km further on.

Avalanche Lake

About 23–25 km · forest permit needed

Pristine shola forest and lake; visits run by the Forest Department eco-tourism jeep.

Pykara Lake & Falls

About 20 km · 45 min

Boating on a calm reservoir and a short walk to the cascading falls.

Coonoor

About 20 km from Ooty, roughly 1 hour by road, or take the toy train for the scenic route. Distances below are from Coonoor town.

Sim's Park

About 4 km

A hillside botanical park with rare trees from around the world, a small lake and shaded walking paths. Famous for its fruit show in May.

Lamb's Rock

About 8 km · short walk from the road

A dramatic cliff-edge viewpoint over tea estates and the Coimbatore plains, on the way to Dolphin's Nose.

One of the Nilgiris' best-known viewpoints, with sweeping valley views and Catherine Falls in the distance on a clear day.

Kotagiri

About 30 km from Ooty via the Doddabetta road. The quietest of the Nilgiri hill towns, with endless tea estates. Distances below are from Kotagiri town.

Kodanad Viewpoint

About 18 km

A wide valley panorama over the eastern Nilgiris, with views of Rangaswamy Peak and Catherine Falls. Go in the morning for clear skies, and try a chai at the stalls.

Open in Google Maps →

Catherine Falls

About 7 km

A tall, two-tiered waterfall, often called the second-highest in the Nilgiris. Best after the monsoon.

Open in Google Maps →

Longwood Shola

About 3 km

A rare patch of native shola forest, great for gentle walks and bird watching.

Open in Google Maps →

Elk Falls

About 7 km

A quieter waterfall set among tea gardens, an easy add-on to Catherine Falls.

Open in Google Maps →

John Sullivan Memorial

Kannerimukku · near town

The restored home of the founder of Ooty, now a museum on Nilgiris history and tribal culture.

Rangaswamy Peak & Pillar

About 25 km

A striking rock pillar and peak sacred to the Irula community, best seen from Kodanad or on a guided trek.
