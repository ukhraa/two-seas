Guest Rules

  1. Smoking is strictly prohibited within the premises.
  2. Turn off the water heater and lights when not in use.
  3. Keep noise low, especially during quiet hours (10 pm – 6 am).
  4. Please keep the main door closed at all times.
  5. Do not dry clothes on the balcony. Ask us about laundry.
  6. Report any breakage; charges apply.
  7. Do not write on the walls or furnishings.
  8. Do not remove cutlery or other items from the apartment.
  9. Only registered guests may stay overnight. Please inform us before inviting visitors.
  10. Bachelor and bachelorette parties, or similar events, are not permitted.
  11. Please carry a valid government photo ID for every adult guest at check-in.
  12. Pets are not allowed at the residence.
  13. Do not feed wild animals or leave food outside; the Nilgiris are home to bison, monkeys and other wildlife.
  14. Ooty is plastic-free; single-use plastics are banned.
  15. Guests will be asked to vacate without a refund if we suspect any illegal activity, and Ooty Police will be informed.
  16. Feel free to knock at our door 24 × 7 for any emergency.
